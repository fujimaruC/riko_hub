## How to record a proposed deviation

For each later entry record: ID; exact source path/panel and traceability ID; current owner-approved requirement; reason; proposed visual and behavior changes; offline/feature/accessibility/performance impact; affected files/phases; reviewable evidence; owner decision/date/scope; accepted remediation and resulting acceptance criteria. Use OPEN → OWNER APPROVED / OWNER REJECTED → VERIFIED CLOSED. Closure evidence comes from Riko; documentation editing alone is not verification of the app.

Approval statuses in the manifest are separate from register workflow states. A technical proposal does not change CANDIDATE to APPROVED. The owner may approve a requirement, image region or explicit fallback without approving unrelated content. Record that scope precisely.
