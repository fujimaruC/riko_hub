## Asset-specific implementation challenges and provenance

All raster artwork is high-resolution and visually detailed. It may be expensive to decode or scale on mobile; any eventual use needs bounded image sizes, a native-friendly asset strategy, and review of offline availability. The mockups also contain small text embedded in images, which must never substitute for accessible live UI text. Typography appears to mix rounded UI lettering with handwritten decorative lines, but no font identity, license, or scale behavior is established. Contrast and screen-reader labeling need implementation review. The source images are supplied references; the origin/license of embedded illustrations is not established here.

## Reading and implementation limits

- A1–A4 are candidate Space concepts. Their numeric suffixes identify files only; they do not imply approval, version order, or priority. The confirmed Space names are Academics, Projects, Personal, and Devices; their order, availability, and final layouts remain open.
- The preferred foundation is **Graphite & Tangerine**, with the owner's non-negotiable principles preserved: warm, playful yet mature, readable at useful density, distinctive per page, organic and tactile, meaningful custom navigation motion, and lightweight native offline behavior.
- Today and the creative Timeline are provisionally approved concepts, recorded as **CANDIDATE**, not `APPROVED`. The owner-approved Task Workspace requirements are an animated lava-like organic header and an expanding task surface. Its source image is missing, so no image-specific geometry can be inferred.
- Treat displayed names, dates, study/health/finance statistics, Git activity, scans, audio capture, device connectivity, battery state, and sync state as image content only. Verify any product data or capability against the current product source before using it.
- Still images show only static frames. They do not establish animation, exact dimensions, color values, typefaces, responsive behavior, accessibility, or interaction. Those details remain proposals unless separately specified by the owner.
- Navigation differs across the supplied concepts: A5 shows Today/Plan/Capture/Vault/Spaces; A1 shows Today/Plan/Spaces/Capture/Vault; A2 shows Today/Spaces/Capture/Vault/Link; A3 shows Home/Plan/Spaces/Vault/Link; A4 shows Today/Spaces/central plus/Capture/Vault/Link. This is an unresolved owner decision, not a choice made by these references.
