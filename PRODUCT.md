# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users

Students who balance coursework with personal obligations, organization work, and longer projects. The exact audience and student contexts may broaden over time; the app is currently designed phone-first.

## Product Purpose

Riko Hub helps students capture obligations and see what matters now, with the current focus on reducing the mental effort of keeping academic, personal, organization, and project work in view. The owner expects the product to become a broader productivity workspace in the future; its scope is intentionally open to development.

## Positioning

The current product brings daily focus, task planning, academic context, personal tasks, selected materials, and optional Linux handoff into one Android app. Its present emphasis is a useful student planner; broader flexible workspace capabilities are a future direction, not a current promise.

## Operating Context

Students capture work on Android, review daily priorities and timelines, group work by course or project, manage reminders, and return to tasks later. Current source also includes Google Drive materials workflows and an optional paired Linux Companion for selected handoff. Connectivity and feature maturity vary; local task planning is the core current use.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Current source includes local tasks, checklists, deadlines, routines and reminders, daily focus and timeline views, course and assignment foundations, project ideas, a personal journal, manual backup, Google Drive browsing and file actions, and explicit device pairing and handoff. These describe source capabilities at initialization, not a guarantee of release readiness or runtime behavior. Scope and integrations may evolve; no fixed long-term feature set or additional product constraints were confirmed. Existing user records remain user-owned and changes to their stored representation should preserve them or migrate them deliberately. Remote access, automatic clipboard sharing, and live collaboration have no current product commitment.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Riko Hub. Current app copy and academic terminology are Indonesian-oriented. Audience, language support, and broader positioning may evolve.

## Product Principles

- Help students see what matters now.
- Keep the initial job focused on capturing and organizing obligations.
- Let the product grow toward broader productivity needs over time.
- Describe present capabilities and maturity honestly.
- Preserve user-owned records as the product evolves.
